# Rocksalt

> Rocksalt is a B2B inbound and social-engagement platform in Redwood City, California, founded in 2023 by Arjun Moorthy (CEO), Anita Moorthy (CMO) and Ajoy Sojan (CTO), and seed-funded by Lightspeed Venture Partners in 2025. It helps founders, executives and subject-matter experts build visibility on LinkedIn and Reddit in about ten minutes a day: an AI digest surfaces the industry conversations worth joining, a "Super You" avatar drafts comments and posts in the user's voice, and the product adds team collaboration, analytics and HubSpot CRM sync. Rocksalt is delivered as an end-user web application and browser extension. It publishes no public API, SDK, webhook surface or developer portal.

## API surface

Rocksalt has no machine-readable API contract. Verified on 2026-08-13 against
every Rocksalt host — www.rocksalt.ai, app.rocksalt.ai, on.rocksalt.ai,
blog.rocksalt.ai and rocksalt.ai:

- No OpenAPI or Swagger document (/openapi.json, /swagger.json, /api-docs all
  404 on the CMS hosts; the two application hosts are single-page apps whose
  catch-all route returns the HTML shell with a 200 for every path).
- No GraphQL endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card.
- No /.well-known/ document of any kind.
- api.rocksalt.ai, docs.rocksalt.ai, developer.rocksalt.ai and
  developers.rocksalt.ai do not resolve in DNS.
- The published sitemap is 37 pages and contains no developer path.
